The organization behind Builder Stack itself. It dogfoods the exact layout every organization at your company follows.

## What lives here

| Folder | What goes in it |
|--------|-----------------|
| [`strategy/`](strategy/) | Org-wide strategy docs and memos |
| [`roadmap/`](roadmap/) | Org-wide roadmaps and planning docs |
| [`teams/`](teams/) | One workspace per team in this organization |

## How it works

1. **Is it about one team?** Put it in that team's workspace instead. Documents here span more than one team.
2. **Pick the folder** that matches the kind of document you're adding.
3. **Create (or open) your author folder** inside it — kebab-case your name, e.g. `ethan-binder/`.
4. **Add your document** as a markdown file named after its title in kebab-case, e.g. `2027-company-bets.md`, opening with `title` / `author` / `date` frontmatter.

See [`organizations/README.md`](../README.md) for how to add a team or another organization.
